## 1. THE HEADLINE

[OPT-4]'s rescue rung is **not neutral and not uniformly good**. Three
measured outcomes, all first-time:

1. **The ctx band and `level-context` win large.** Where the collapsed
   language keeps discriminating structure outside the collapsed repeat, the

3. **Nothing else moved.** 32 of 46 bounded forms, 20 of 22 loglines forms
   and 6 of 6 email forms are `sel=selected`, and every one of their
   numbers reproduces the BEFORE within the box's spread. I-18 (1) holds.

## 7. E. RANKED OPTIMIZATION CANDIDATES (pcrec D86), UPDATED

**NEW — 1. `[a-z]{0,n}`'s count-collapsed prefilter is a net loss and should
not be built.** `cls-upto-32768` / plain: search **×3.57 slower** than the
same pin's forced VM and ×1.90 behind the JIT (was 1.93× ahead);
throughput **6.899 ns/B against 1.930** for the forced VM, ×9.94 on
`t-letters-064k`, ×1.65 even on the digits run the prefilter was supposed
to dismiss on. Mechanism, from the stamps and the subjects: the collapsed
language of a `{0,n}` body is `X*`, which is **nullable**, so the prefilter
admits at every position and can never dismiss; the artifact pays a scan
with no possible win. **This is the sharpest new row in the sample and it
is cheap to fix as a predicate**: build the collapsed prefilter only when
the collapsed language is non-nullable (equivalently, when something
outside the collapsed repeat survives the collapse). Bounded brackets it
exactly: the four ctx rungs and `level-context` — where a literal
alternation survives — win 2.2–4.6×; the three `cls-*` rungs — where nothing
survives — lose 1.2–9.9×. Ten labelled points, one predicate.

VM is faster — now chartered as [OPT-5], and the knee is NOT on the count.**

**What the added rungs must resolve.** On the letters axis the DFA already
loses 5.1× at n=256, the lowest rung the bench has — so **either the knee is
below 256, or there is no count knee on that axis at all** and the choice
should key on the subject/language rather than the count. On the digits axis
the DFA still wins 1.75× at n=16384 — so on that axis the knee, if it
exists, is **above** 16384. bounded@0.2's intermediate rungs (both letter
and digit throughput subjects, both forms) are what separate "a knee at some
n" from "two different answers for two different subjects", and that is the
distinction [OPT-5] has to make. A rung at n = 64 or 128 would be worth
adding for the same reason: it is the only way to bracket a knee below 256.

**The group-vs-class rung** (`[a-z]{0,1024}`, ask (vi) of O-9) now has a
sharper target than the size question it was proposed for: `grp-upto-1024`'s
DFA is **5.76× behind the VM on letters and 0.572 ahead on digits** —
numerically indistinguishable from the pure class rungs at 256/4096/16384
(5.14–5.86 / 0.567–0.581). If `[a-z]{0,1024}` reproduces those figures, the
group body costs pcrec nothing at run time and the whole group-vs-class term
is a compile-size story (`.so` 43,144 vs an interpolated ≈ 35,000 B;
emitted C 80,235 vs an interpolated ≈ 60,000 B).
