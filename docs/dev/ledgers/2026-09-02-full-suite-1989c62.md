### 2.4 The mechanism — CONFIRMED FROM pcrec's OWN RECORD, not inferred

The reports lane's hypothesis was that [CC-CLANG] step 1's `has_push`
gate omits the frameless fail-label dispatcher. **pcrec has already
recorded the finding and the mechanism**, read-only in `~/pcrec`:

- `docs/dev/plan.md`, row `[CC-CLANG]`, carries an **UNPREDICTED EFFECT**
  paragraph citing this bench's own flag and states the mechanism from
  **`src/gen/emit_vm.c`:9482-9560**: "step 1's `has_push` gate omits the
  whole pop-and-resume block INCLUDING the computed goto on any program
  with no `RX_PUSH` site and no linked call; a function without a
  computed goto lets gcc optimize the scan loop whole-function
  (regalloc/loop opts), which dead-code removal alone would not
  explain." It is filed for I-31 and explicitly "not a charter goal, not
  measured on our side".
- The gate itself is at `src/gen/emit_vm.c`:
  `const bool has_push = v.emitted_push || v.has_linked_calls;` — with a
  comment recording that an earlier `strstr` form of the same gate was
  refuted by their battery, and that `v.npush` is deliberately not
  consulted (the unbounded-counter arm went negative and caused the
  2026-09-01 tier-1 miscompile, `adc0f5a`/`ae3e6ca`).

**What this ledger can conclude**: our `resume_frames == 1` stamp is the
bench-visible proxy for pcrec's `has_push == false` — every artifact in
the −402 bucket has a frame capacity of one, and none of the ≥ 2 bucket
moved. **What it cannot**: we cannot see the emitted text, so we cannot
say the −402 B *is* the dispatcher, nor separate w12's 519 B/artifact
comment fix (which moves every artifact downward) from abi 15's two
fields (upward) inside the net. Only pcrec can. Our contribution is the
population, the controls and the size of the effect.

### 2.5 The finding is GCC-specific — a second, independent corroboration

From the cc axis (§5, REC): on `bounded@0.3` × `pcrec-vm`, `floor` /
`large-subject-throughput` reads **31,574.6 ns/call under gcc and
63,018.2 under clang — clang is ×2.00 SLOWER** and is the single worst
clang cell in the whole night. The frameless win is a whole-function
optimisation gcc performs on a scan loop with no computed goto; **clang
does not perform it**, on the same emitted C. That the toolchain which
FORCED the gate to exist is the one that does not reap its accidental
benefit is worth a sentence in O-14.

### 2.6 The trade: what the frameless change costs

The win is not free, and the split is by what the VM's inner loop is
doing (REC, XV, forced-VM arm, per-pattern median ratio 1989c62 ÷
a7e0bdf):

| VM work | example | ratio | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| pure FAILING scan | `floor` thr | **0.111** | ×9.0 faster |
| pure FAILING scan | `cls-atleast-4096` on digits thr | **0.364** (20,001.0 → 7,271.8) | ×2.75 faster |
| pure FAILING scan | `floor` search | 0.219 | ×4.6 faster |
| MATCHING whole-subject | `cls-upto-*` match | **0.765–0.803** | ×1.25 faster |
| EMPTY-match-and-advance | `cls-upto-*` on digits thr | **1.059–1.071** | **×1.07 SLOWER** |
| EMPTY-match-and-advance | `cls-upto-*` plain search | **1.084–1.093** | **×1.09 SLOWER** |

So: **failing scans and matching whole-subject calls got faster; the

stated rather than averaged away.

## 4. THE LOW RUNGS AND THE SHORT-RUN FAMILY (I-29 ask (ii))

### 4.1 P2 — the boundary IS a stamp, and the ladder brackets it at k = 2–4

**P2 is answered and it is the SECOND, cheaper answer**: pcrec declines
the edge below a boundary on the bounded spelling, and the ladder
brackets that boundary at **k = 2 … 4** (`\d{1,2}` declines, `\d{1,4}`
takes it). It is not "extract at every rung and let economics decide".

### 4.2 P3 — REFUTED, in both directions, which NOTES.md called the most interesting outcome available

At the same k the fixed and bounded spellings carry DIFFERENT edge
values, and the direction flips with the form:

- **plain, k = 2**: `\d{2}` → `range`, `\d{1,2}` → `none`.
- **whole-subject, every k**: the entire fixed family → `none`, while
  `\d{1,4}` and up → `range`.

So the edge is not a property of the class run alone; the pattern's
spelling and the artifact's entry form both enter the decision. P3 said
"a DIFFERING edge value across a pair is a finding" — it is one.

### 4.3 The size of the term (O-12 ask (ii)'s open half: "is the fixed term's size known?")
