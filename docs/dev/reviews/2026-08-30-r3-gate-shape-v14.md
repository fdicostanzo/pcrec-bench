## Lessons

A rule's constants can be measured correctly and still be the wrong
SHAPE: the row census was right to the row and the fraction it fed was
quantised by a unit the census had not named (the group). Three
critics with different instruments found the same three holes from
different sides (the trial count nobody recorded, the sentence nobody
kept first, the row nobody defined), which is what makes a panel worth
its cost. And a ruling written against one file's numbers (R-19,
R-20) meets a different number when the arithmetic changes underneath
it — recompute before applying, and escalate the difference rather than
smoothing it.
