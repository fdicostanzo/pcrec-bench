ITEM 1 predictions:

1a. "varwidth and neg on any subject with sparse candidates are x2-x21
    faster" -- CONFIRMED IN DIRECTION, MAGNITUDE FAR EXCEEDED on the
    real (non-density-tuned) corpus. I-114's own synthetic subjects
    (synth-64k-asc, synth-1m) land roughly IN the predicted x2-x21
    band on varwidth (1.17-3.95x) and inside-or-near it on `fixed`
    (0.49-1.04x, near flat). But on `bench/utf8`'s own REAL throughput
    prose (t-64k/t-256k/t-1m/t-64k-asc/t-64k-lat -- ordinary mixed
    text, not density-tuned) the speedup is 11x-99x, an order of
    magnitude past the predicted ceiling. The mechanism is consistent
    with I-124's own axis-table wording ("the compile's byte-rate
    prior puts the candidate scan's byte set at a mean gap under the
    class's calibrated crossover: adaptive... else a step"): I-114's
    hand-twin subjects were deliberately tuned to one candidate
    density; ordinary prose has its own uncontrolled, often HIGHER
    failed-candidate density, and the pre-abi-48 FIXED behaviour
    re-seeds (re-scans the whole remaining subject from the prefilter)
    after EVERY failed candidate -- a cost that compounds with
    candidate count in a way the density-tuned synthetic subjects
    could not surface at the same scale. **This is the headline
    finding**: the real fix's win on ordinary text is much larger than
    I-114's own Mac scratch measurement predicted, not merely in the
    predicted range.
1b. "fixed/synth-dense is flat (+-5%)" -- CONFIRMED. gcc: denied/
    default = 1.042 (4.2% denied slower, i.e. default 4.0% slower than
    denied read the other way -- within the +-5% band either reading);
    clang: 0.967 (3.3% inside the band).
1c. "CLAMPED rows do not move" -- NOT TESTED by this probe (none of
    the five patterns measured here carries the `clamped` row; Phase
    A's own census puts `clamped` on `bounded`'s ctx-*/nest* family,
    `capability`'s logparse-* family and `loglines`'s `level-context`
    -- all outside this probe's two groups. Item 3's real window, not
    yet run, is where this sub-claim gets tested).

ITEM 2 predictions (syntax@0.1 lka-pos/lka-verb):

2a. "auto no longer equals forced-VM" -- CONFIRMED. Every one of the
    six (pattern, subject) cells reads auto/forced-vm away from 1.0
    (0.33-1.90), in BOTH directions depending on subject size.
2b. "faster on sparse throughput subjects; slower (SLOWER, the
    XCALL trigger) on a match-dense one" -- NOT CLEANLY TESTABLE WITH
    THESE SUBJECTS, recorded as such rather than forced to a verdict.
    `bench/syntax`'s own t-64k/t-256k/t-1m are the SAME grammar at
    three independently-drawn sizes, not a sparse/dense CONTROLLED
    pair the way bounded/utf8 sets carry one -- so "sparse" vs
    "match-dense" cannot be read off subject choice here. The data
    itself does not even show a clean size-monotonic trend: `lka-verb`
    reads auto FASTER than forced-vm at all three sizes (0.75/0.33/
    0.43); `lka-pos` reads auto SLOWER at the two smaller sizes
    (1.47/1.90) and FASTER at the largest (0.47). A real test of 2b
    needs a subject pair with CONTROLLED match/candidate density (the
    way `bench/bounded`'s short-run family or a purpose-built subject
    would give), not a size ladder over one grammar.

ITEM 3 (the roster-wide >5%-slower census): this probe's own five
patterns ARE part of item 3's population (Phase A's census), so their
cells count. Reading them against the >5%-slower-than-denied rule:
**no cell with real candidate traffic reads default more than 5%
SLOWER than denied** -- every such cell reads default FASTER (the
whole speedup column above, and GROUP S's denied/auto column, all
exceed 1.05 wherever there is real work). The only cells where default
reads slower at all are the near-timer-floor ones named above
(asr-lb-neg on t-64k-lat/cyr/asc, 1.1-3.0 us absolute, ratio 0.46-0.56,
i.e. default ~1.8-2.2x "slower" by ratio but on a cell doing almost no
real work) -- flagged, not hidden, and not treated as a genuine
[OPT-HYB-RESEED-XCALL] trigger per this project's own measurement
discipline (a ratio at the timer floor is not evidence). **Item 3's
full roster window (bounded/capability/loglines's own clamped and
adaptive-dense population, not reachable from this probe's two groups)
is still owed to the manager's real `run_suite.sh` window** --
