## 6. The one no-hit-background comparison that DOES exist, and why it
   points the OPPOSITE way from R4's ratio

  alt-cyr-64 on t-64k-asc (pure ASCII, 0 possible hits -- cited from
  docs/dev/measurements/2026-09-26-utf8-0.1-r2-r7-addendum-extract.txt
  lines 206/222, not recomputed here):
    libpcre2-dfa    : 1.327585 ns/B
    libpcre2-interp : 1.334520 ns/B

  w-64 on t-128k-clean (pure run/prose, 0 branch occurrences), 131072 B,
  ns/byte = median_ns / 131072 (this report has no ns_per_byte column,
  computed here from the same 'rank'/median_ns row the report prints):
    rust_1.13.1_default-caps-simdna          2.384500 ns/B  (312541.2 ns total)
    pcrec_25b1984f_auto-caps-simdna          3.674676 ns/B  (481647.2 ns total)
    pcrec_25b1984f_auto-nocaps-simdna        3.691265 ns/B  (483821.5 ns total)
    pcrec_25b1984f_vm-in-caps-simdna         14.342083 ns/B  (1879845.4 ns total)
    pcrec_25b1984f_vm-caps-simdna            14.365822 ns/B  (1882957.1 ns total)
    libpcre2_10.46_jit-caps-simdna           36.777829 ns/B  (4820543.6 ns total)
    libpcre2_10.46_interp-caps-simdna        623.851845 ns/B  (81769509.0 ns total)

  So on their respective all-miss backgrounds, alt-cyr-64 (2 lead bytes,
  0xd0/0xd1, IMPOSSIBLE in ASCII text) is ~2-3 orders of magnitude
  CHEAPER per byte than w-64 (26-letter bitmap, common in English prose)
  on libpcre2-interp -- the opposite direction from R4's ×420/×228. This
  is explained by candidate-start SELECTIVITY (2 possible lead bytes vs
  26), not by Cyrillic-vs-ASCII per se; altwide's own sh1-*(1 lead byte)/
  nar4-*(4)/w-*(26) structure-arm family already brackets this axis, so a
  same-branch-count ASCII control at exactly 2 lead bytes would land
  INSIDE that already-charted family, not open new machinery.
